## Fresh evidence

[`../results/2026-10-06_0018_windows/EVIDENCE.md`](../results/2026-10-06_0018_windows/EVIDENCE.md)
records three repetitions for 128/512/2048 plus diagnostic 8192, with 32 shared teacher-forced decode steps.
CUDA accelerates primary prefill by 40.00-77.79x and decode by 9.16-9.76x versus the current CPU reference.
All three CPU denominators are reported; none is silently substituted for another.

The ordinary CUDA-build CPU control lacks the CPU_REPACK model buffer. `no_host=true` restores its size to
514,326,528 bytes, matching the CPU reference, and restores bit identity at all eight saved checkpoints.
This supports the proposed buffer/repacking mechanism; sequential measurements do not isolate small timing
differences or establish kernel-level causality. The previous dated evidence package remains unchanged.
