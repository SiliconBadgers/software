Problem: A train leaves the station at 9:15 and travels at 72 kilometres per hour. A second train leaves the same station at 9:45 on a parallel track and travels at 90 kilometres per hour in the same direction. At what time does the second train catch up with the first, and how far from the station are they then?

Solution: When the second train departs, the first has already been travelling for 30 minutes, which is half an hour, so it is 72 * 0.5 = 36 kilometres ahead. The second train closes the gap at a rate of 90 - 72 = 18 kilometres per hour. The time needed to close 36 kilometres is 36 / 18 = 2 hours. Two hours after 9:45 is 11:45. In that time the second train covers 90 * 2 = 180 kilometres, and the first train, travelling for 2.5 hours in total, covers 72 * 2.5 = 180 kilometres as well, which confirms the answer. They meet at 11:45, 180 kilometres from the station.
